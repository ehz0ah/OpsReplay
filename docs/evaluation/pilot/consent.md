# Participant information and consent

OpsReplay is a student project for practising software incident response. We ask you
to study two incidents, answer short questions, use a web application and discuss its
usability. The session takes about 65 minutes. Participation is voluntary. It does not
affect grades, employment or access to your own learning materials. You may stop at any
time or skip a question.

## What is collected

The facilitator records your pseudonym, experience, answers, study timings, assistance
use and usability feedback. During an interactive Challenge, OpsReplay records terminal
input and output, command markers, service metrics, logs and watched configuration
captures. It stores the outcome, debrief and attempt history. Your account identifier
links these records. Use only the provided test environment. Do not paste passwords,
personal files, work data or real customer information.

The assistant is off during the two core conditions. No core study content goes to an
LLM provider. Optional assistant testing is excluded from this consent and needs a
separate explanation of the selected provider and transmitted fields.

## Access, retention and deletion

Only the authorised project team accesses identifiable study records. Published
reports use anonymous scores and redacted examples. There is no public recording link.

Live recording objects have a seven-day retention rule. Sealed recordings have a
30-day rule. Application playback access ends at its saved retention boundary. Managed
storage expiration can happen later. Debriefs, account records and study files do not
automatically follow recording expiry.

Before consent, the facilitator must give you the named deletion owner, contact address,
fixed study-file deletion date, and current backup expiry. These values belong in your
private consent copy. Do not start if they are absent.

You may request withdrawal of identifiable study data before anonymisation. The team
first ends and finalises an active Challenge, then deletes its live application session
data and recording. It removes your private responses and study exports separately.
The session deletion action does not delete your Cognito identity or Code Review
submission; the owner handles those separately if requested. Managed backups may retain
old data until their configured expiry and are not individually edited. After the
identity mapping is removed, anonymous aggregate results may no longer be traceable to
you and cannot reliably be withdrawn.

## Consent record

The facilitator stores this record privately, separate from answers:

- I understand the purpose, collected data, retention and deletion limits.
- I know whom to contact and the fixed study-file deletion date.
- I know that participation is voluntary and that I may stop.
- I agree to the described recording and study. A Challenge cannot run without its
  recording, so an alternative non-recorded static activity is available.

Record consent as yes or no, with date and pseudonym. Do not commit signatures, names,
contact addresses or completed forms to the public repository.
